Germany Campaign • Alternate WW2 Timeline

You’ll face strategic decisions across diplomacy, production, air/sea war, and fronts. Choices update the world state, unlock different questions, and lead to multiple endings.

How it works

Every question has 4 choices. Each choice changes stats like Fuel, Morale, Industry, and Front strength. The next decision is selected based on your situation, not a fixed script—so runs feel different.

Goal

Reach decision 100 with strong fronts, stable resources, and manageable war weariness—or negotiate an armistice. If morale or fronts collapse, the campaign ends early.
